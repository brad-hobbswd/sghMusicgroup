Our Story

Built Around the Message.

SGH Music Group is a place where artists are more than names. They are family, connected by a shared purpose to spread the Good News of Jesus through song.

Founder & Radio Promoter

Stephanie Hager

SGH Promotions was formed in 2023 through an inspiration that only God can give. With years of experience in radio promotion, Stephanie's desire has remained clear: help artists carry the Gospel further while keeping God at the center.

The mission is not recognition alone. It is impact. Every song is an opportunity to point someone to the Cross and remind them that Jesus is right for whatever is wrong in their life.

Our Foundation

Serving God Honestly

SGH Music Group believes ministry and excellence belong together. The work exists to encourage artists, support opportunities, and create pathways for Gospel music to reach more people.

“Keep God at the center of the work, and let the message remain greater than the moment.”
